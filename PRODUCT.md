# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React (static build, deployable to any static host). Motion via CSS / Web Animations API and the `motion` library where interruptible, physics-based motion is needed.

## Users

Women planning an occasion (evening events, galas, engagements, weddings) who want a one-of-a-kind, made-to-order gown. They usually discover Glamlove through Instagram, browse on a phone, and want to see the craft up close before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

Glamlove is a couture atelier: gowns are made to order. The site presents the atelier's work and turns interest into a private consultation request over WhatsApp. Success is a visitor sending a WhatsApp message to book a fitting or consultation.

## Positioning

Hand-draped, made-to-measure couture shown on the atelier's own mannequins: the pleating, draping and construction visible in the photographs are the proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Made-to-order couture only (not a shop, no cart, no prices).
- Primary contact channel: WhatsApp. The number is not yet provided; it lives in one config constant to replace.
- Instagram handle: `@glamlove_couture` (the photo watermarks read `@glamlove.couture`; confirm which is current).
- Undecided: location/address, opening hours, lead times, pricing. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Glamlove (Glamlove Couture). Logo: "Glamlove" high-contrast serif wordmark with a butterfly mark above it.
- The butterfly is the brand mark; the loading moment features it flying and landing above the wordmark.
- User-pinned look: clean, modern, classy, white canvas. No gradients, no typical AI-generated patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

- `src/assets/looks/`: 11 photographs supplied by the owner (atelier mannequin gowns: teal halter mermaid gown front / with stole / back; ivory taffeta draped mermaid gown; plus black feather-cuff gown, burgundy crystal midi, red strapless midi, ivory sculpted-collar dress, Missoni-style knit maxis). Owner confirmed rights to publish all 11.
- Logo source: a phone screenshot of the round logo (traced to SVG in `src/components/Logo*`).
- No testimonials, press, client names, prices or awards exist; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The garments lead; the interface recedes.
2. Every page ends one tap from a WhatsApp conversation.
3. Restraint over decoration: motion and type carry the luxury, not effects.
4. Never claim what the atelier has not confirmed.
